ACADEMIC PROJECT OVERVIEW

Digital Marketing & SEO Practical Implementation

This website is constructed as an active live laboratory for testing modern Search Engine Optimization strategies. It fulfills practical requirements for:

1. Keyword Research & Intent Targeted primary keyword: "healthy diet for college students" alongside high-opportunity informational long-tail keywords.
2. On-Page Content Optimization Optimized Title tags (<60 chars), meta descriptions (<160 chars), H1-H3 structural hierarchies, and keyword-rich image ALT tags.
3. Technical SEO & Schema Dynamic XML sitemaps, robots.txt, canonical URLs, and JSON-LD structured data (Schema.org/BlogPosting).
4. Google Search Console & Tools Equipped with meta verification tag placeholders for rapid indexing checks in GSC and crawl audits in Ubersuggest / Screaming Frog.
E-E-A-T COMPLIANCE

Evidence-Based, Fluff-Free Guidelines

Health and wellness advice falls under Google's YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) quality framework. That is why every guide on FitPulse adheres to strict journalistic standards:

  • ✓ No unverified "miracle detoxes" or overpriced commercial powders.
  • ✓ Realistic dorm hacks using standard campus dining and grocery staples.
  • ✓ Clear internal link pathways enabling easy reader navigation.
